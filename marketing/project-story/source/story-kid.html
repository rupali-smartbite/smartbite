01 / THE PROBLEM

A small packet.
A big secret.

Packaged snacks and drinks are everywhere in Indian tiffins and evenings. The truth about them is hidden in tiny print, per 100 g, that almost nobody reads.

ONE 250 ML LEMON-LIME SODA
24.5 g

of sugar. That's about 6 teaspoons.

🥄🥄🥄🥄🥄🥄

Almost an adult's whole daily limit for added sugar, in a few sips.

ONE SMALL PACKET OF CHIPS
⅓

of a child's daily salt.

Before lunch, dinner or any other snack of the day. Kids' bodies need far less salt than adults'.

Hard to readLabels show values per 100 g, not per packet, so the real amount is hidden.
Made for adultsDaily limits on packs are for grown-ups. Kids need their own verdict.
No desi answerEven when we know it's junk, what should we eat instead?
So we built SmartBite.Scan before you snack. Free for every family.
