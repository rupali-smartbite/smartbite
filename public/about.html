The problem

Food labels are hard to read, and packaged snacks and sugary drinks are part of everyday life for many Indian children and families. At the same time, lifestyle diseases are rising.

What SmartBite does

  • Scan a packet by barcode or photo, or search by name.
  • Scan a plate: a photo of a thali, street food or fruit.
  • Two verdicts: one for adults and one for children (Good, Okay in moderation, or Avoid), with simple reasons.
  • Junk food meter from 0 to 7, and an explanation of every additive and E-number.
  • Healthier swaps for breakfast, lunch and dinner, for veg, egg and non-veg, plus a swap finder for any dish.
  • Bitu, a friendly food helper that answers in English, Hindi or Marathi.
  • Play & Learn games, a daily tracker, SmartBite Coins and a healthy-food shop (payments coming soon).
  • Hardware version: an ESP32 device with a screen and LED lights for school canteens, no phone needed.

How a verdict is worked out

The same product always gets the same verdict. SmartBite finds the nutrition values in this order:

  1. Verified Indian database: real Indian pack values for popular brands. Recipes differ by country: Sprite has 9.8 g sugar per 100 ml in India but 3.3 g in the UK, so Indian values matter.
  2. Numbers read from the back of the pack, used only when they add up (sugar not more than carbs, calories matching fat, carbs and protein).
  3. Open Food Facts, the world's open food database, with Indian packs first.
  4. AI estimate, clearly marked "verify with pack".

The score is then calculated by fixed rules, never by the AI, using WHO and ICMR-NIN daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat. Children get stricter limits, and drinks with caffeine are always "Avoid" for children. Data typing errors are corrected before scoring.

Privacy and safety

  • Your name, profile, history and coins stay only on your own phone, and are wiped when you log out.
  • The server stores no health data and no IP addresses.
  • No passwords or secret keys are written in the code.
  • The site blocks injected scripts, cannot be embedded by other sites, and always uses HTTPS.
  • SmartBite is for awareness, not medical advice. For health conditions, please ask a doctor or dietitian.
